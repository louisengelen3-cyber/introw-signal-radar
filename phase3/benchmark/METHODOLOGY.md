# Phase 3 control sets — methodology

**Frozen:** 23 Aug 2026, before the positive constructs were run against any of them.

## Why new sets were needed

The Phase 3 label-provenance audit found that **13 of 14 Cohort C negative labels cite
distribution structure in their own rationale**, and that Cohort B was sampled from
distributor lists. Measuring distributor-carriage against those labels partly measures my
own priors. Only one Phase 2 negative (Deloitte) survives as independent.

## CLEAN STRUCTURAL NEGATIVES

A company qualifies only if its negative rationale is **independent of every feature the
positive model tests** — no appeal to distribution, no appeal to programme complexity, no
appeal to size or reputation.

Admissible negative rationales:

- **participant_only** — the company's partner language describes membership of another
  vendor's programme. It is a channel participant, and Introw is bought by operators.
- **integration_only** — the ecosystem is technical; there is no commercial partner motion
  to operate. Verified as such in Phase 1 measurement.
- **affiliate_only** — a link-attributed performance-marketing motion, a different buyer
  and a different object model.
- **professional_services_partner** — "partner" denotes an equity partner.
- **no_indirect_motion** — the company sells only direct and publishes no partner motion.

Inadmissible: "multi-tier", "distributor-led", "enterprise", "too large", "complex
incentives". Those are the features under test.

## MATCHED UNLABELLED PROSPECTS

For selected known customers, a comparable company matched on product category, segment,
geography, rough stage and GTM shape — **not** matched on the positive features under test.

**These carry no fit label.** A company that is not a known Introw customer is not a
negative: it may be an excellent unconverted prospect, a live opportunity, or a future
customer. They are used to inspect generalisation and to see what a real candidate set
would look like — never as negatives, and never scored as right or wrong.

## KNOWN-CUSTOMER HOLDOUT

The 19 customers with retrievable partner pages split deterministically: the 12 whose
pages informed hypothesis generation are the discovery set, and the remaining 7 are held
out. Both are reported separately, and neither is treated as a clean negative's opposite.
