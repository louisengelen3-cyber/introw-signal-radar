A blind reviewer, handed nothing but the machine's own evidence packets with every company name redacted, made the right call on six of six hard negatives. The automated scoring, reading the identical evidence, promoted five of six — every one of them a direct Introw competitor.

The two bold rows decide the product. The model promotes Introw's competitors at a higher rate than it promotes Introw's prospects. GitLab, Freshworks, Pipedrive and Datadog — all running substantial real partner programmes — were not promoted. Magentrix, Mindmatrix and Zift Solutions were.

Why it fails

Promotion is a function of how much a company publishes about partner programmes. PRM vendors publish more of it than anyone alive, because for them partner language is simultaneously the product copy and the go-to-market copy.

Publication-volume confound · n=28

Mean observation count: 10.50 promoted versus 3.78 not promoted. Of the ten companies clearing the ≥ 9 threshold, nine are partner-tech vendors.

  1. The constructs fire on partner-programme language.
  2. Language volume determines observation count.
  3. Observation count ≥ 9 determines promotion.
  4. Partner-tech vendors maximise partner-programme language.
  5. Therefore the promotion signal is, structurally, a partner-tech-vendor detector.

This is not a bug to be patched. It is what the instrument measures. Operational ownership was not wrong about the competitors — they genuinely do operate their own programmes, directly. The construct answered accurately, and the answer was irrelevant to the question being asked.

The repair, and its honest failure

Having identified the two detectable mechanisms — second-person voice (“grow your partner program”) and definitional glossary content — I built a competitor detector keyed on self-description, grammatical person and URL section shape. No domain list, per the mandate's prohibition on patching company names.

Then I froze a set it had never seen and ran it.

Competitor detectorIn-sampleOut-of-sample
Partner-tech vendors excluded3 / 61 / 6
False positives on real operators00 / 6

The 3/6 → 1/6 collapse is the signature of a rule written after seeing the answers. Perfect precision, near-zero recall — the expected shape when a detector is fitted to six examples. ziftsolutions.com, a PRM vendor with 11 observations, is still promoted to high_fit and still reads as clear. This is why the holdout was frozen before the run.

People · public web only

16 of 18 companies returned unknown. The single company satisfying TWO_PLUS was impartner.com — a PRM vendor whose site is dense with named partner-professional testimonials. The publication-volume confound again, not a partner team we discovered.

Three extractor bugs surfaced this sprint in code that had already shipped through three phases. Site navigation was read as a person — axon.com returned the “individual” Browse Partner, title Directory Become a Partner, because Director matched inside Directory. And programme-path matching read /glossary/reseller as a programme surface because it tested substrings rather than the first path segment. Both are fixed and covered by tests. Assume more remain.

Product decision

Evidence Assistant

Not Research Radar MVP — the ranking is measurably inverted on the hardest class, and shipping a ranked queue teaches sellers to distrust it. Not Public-Data Limit — that verdict is refuted by the blind review: a human reading only machine-collected packets got the hardest calls right, so the data is sufficient and the scoring is what fails.

Ships

  • Evidence dossiers: observed quotes, source URLs, and proves / doesNotProve on every line
  • Explicit notObserved and retrievalProblems — the reviewer used these to separate “searched and found nothing” from “could not look”, and it changed decisions
  • States, not scores
  • Competitor flag as review_required, never automatic exclusion
  • Human decides. Every time.

Does not ship

  • high_fit as a ranked output
  • CRM as a qualification gate
  • Person-based qualification
  • Timing signals, until the temporal store holds months rather than hours

GTM Queue — no

Not now, and not after review. A queue implies an ordering, and the ordering is the part that failed. What can go to sellers is a research dossier on companies they are already considering — which is where the measured value sits: the reviewer produced three callable prospects with quotable hooks from redacted evidence alone. Revisit only if a prospectively frozen holdout shows operators promoting above competitors. That has never yet been observed.

Corrections

Phase 3's “0/14 clean negatives falsely promoted” stands, but was over-read. It measured easy negatives. Against hard negatives the same constructs promote 5/6. The result was never wrong; it was quoted as evidence for something it did not test.

My own in-sample “6/6 with zero false positives” was inflated. It used an OR of two signals. Under the two-indicator rule inherited from the Phase 1 firm-type fix, the real in-sample figure is 3/6 — and out-of-sample, 1/6.

This sprint is complete and the project stops here. No Phase 5 is proposed. The open items — a prospectively frozen holdout, a re-run blind review with the introw fingerprint stripped, and temporal observations with real elapsed time — are what would be required, not work now in progress.
